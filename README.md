# GRC Fundamentals: Governance, Risk, and Compliance at Nexus Financial Services

This project explores the fundamentals of **Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC)** through a case study of Nexus Financial Services, a growing financial company based in Lagos, Nigeria. It identifies organizational challenges and proposes a dedicated GRC team to improve governance, manage risks, and ensure compliance with applicable regulations, industry standards, and internal policies.

## Project Overview

The purpose of this project is to demonstrate an understanding of **Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC)** and how these principles can help organizations establish effective controls, manage business risks, and meet regulatory obligations.

The case study focuses on **Nexus Financial Services**, a growing financial company in Lagos, Nigeria, that has experienced significant expansion but lacks an appropriate GRC structure.

### Problems Identified

- **Governance:** No clear process for approving or enforcing company policies.
- **Risk Management:** No standardized approach to assessing risks before making business decisions.
- **Compliance:** No designated person responsible for tracking regulatory requirements and deadlines.

### Project Objectives

- Establish a structured approach to governance, risk management, and compliance.
- Define responsibilities for managing organizational policies and objectives.
- Identify potential risks and communicate them to the board.
- Improve adherence to applicable regulations, industry standards, and internal policies.
- Strengthen accountability and build stakeholder confidence.

### Proposed Solution

Appoint a dedicated **GRC team** to fulfil the responsibilities of a Compliance Analyst, Risk Manager, Internal Auditor, and Privacy Officer.

## GRC Framework

The proposed GRC framework brings together three key components to support the organization's objectives.

### 1. Governance

Governance involves aligning the different parts of an organization toward a common goal.

Key responsibilities include:

- Defining clear organizational goals and objectives.
- Establishing rules and policies to guide daily operations.
- Creating processes for approving and enforcing company policies.
- Promoting accountability in organizational decision-making.

### 2. Risk Management

Risk refers to the possibility that an event or situation could negatively affect an organization's ability to achieve its objectives.

Key responsibilities include:

- Identifying potential business risks.
- Assessing risks before making business decisions.
- Communicating identified risks to the board.
- Supporting decisions about the organization's risk appetite.
- Developing strategies to mitigate identified risks.

### 3. Compliance

Compliance involves ensuring that an organization follows applicable laws, regulations, industry standards, contractual obligations, and internal policies and procedures.

Key responsibilities include:

- Monitoring applicable regulatory requirements and deadlines.
- Ensuring adherence to internal policies and industry standards.
- Maintaining oversight of compliance activities.
- Working with internal audit to support checks and balances.
- Addressing privacy responsibilities through the proposed Privacy Officer role.

## Results and Findings

The project identified significant gaps in the GRC structure of Nexus Financial Services and proposed a dedicated team to address them.

### Key Findings

- **Governance Gaps:** The company lacked clear processes for approving and enforcing policies.
- **Risk Management Gaps:** There was no standardized approach to assessing risks before business decisions.
- **Compliance Gaps:** Responsibility for tracking regulatory requirements and deadlines had not been clearly assigned.
- **Accountability Gaps:** A dedicated GRC team was proposed to establish clearer responsibilities across governance, risk, compliance, audit, and privacy.

### Expected Outcomes

Implementing the proposed GRC framework is expected to help the company:

- Avoid costly risks through improved identification and assessment.
- Improve risk mitigation and organizational decision-making.
- Strengthen adherence to internal policies, regulations, and industry standards.
- Establish clearer accountability for governance and compliance activities.
- Build a stronger reputation and greater confidence among stakeholders.

**Conclusion:** This project demonstrates how a structured GRC framework can help a growing financial organization address governance gaps, manage potential risks, and improve compliance oversight. The proposed GRC team provides a foundation for stronger accountability and more informed business decisions.
